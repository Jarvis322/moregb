The app

More GB asks iOS to release cache it is holding, and reports what the system actually returned. Its photo tools shrink, convert or delete only the photos and videos you choose, after iOS asks you to confirm. Nothing else on your device is changed.

What is and is not promised

No specific amount of space is promised. The figure the app shows is what iOS reports as reclaimable at that moment. How much the system actually releases depends on the version of iOS, on how full the device is, and on decisions Apple makes that are not published. A run may return several gigabytes, and a run may return nothing. The app measures and reports the real result either way, and a result of zero is not a defect.

  • Where a free trial is offered, any unused part of it is forfeited if you buy a subscription during the trial.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in the Settings app under your Apple Account, not in More GB.
  • The one-time purchase does not renew and does not expire.
