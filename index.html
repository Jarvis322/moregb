Lowers Settings

Shrink and clear your photos and videos

Your own photos and videos are the only thing any app can take off the used figure in Settings. More GB finds what takes the most room:

  • Large videos re-encoded to HEVC, or 4K brought down to 1080p
  • ProRAW photos rewritten as full-size HEIC
  • Old Live Photos turned into stills
  • Screenshots, screen recordings, bursts and near-duplicates

Nothing changes until you choose it, and every rewrite keeps the date, place, favourite mark and albums. Deleted items wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days; the app reminds you to empty it.

Questions

Why does my iPhone say storage is full when Settings shows free space?

Settings counts the cache iOS is holding as free, but apps cannot write into it until iOS clears it. A video or an update can run out of room while Settings still looks fine. More GB asks iOS to clear that cache now, so the space is usable.

Can an app clear System Data on iPhone?

No. iOS does not let any app empty System Data or another app's cache, including WhatsApp. More GB does not claim to. What lowers the used figure in Settings is shrinking or deleting photos and videos, then emptying Recently Deleted.

Does More GB delete my photos?

Only the photos and videos you pick, and iOS asks you to confirm every change. Converted items keep their date, place, favourite mark and albums, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted first.

Why did a run return nothing?

iOS decides how much of its cache to release, and it gives up little when the phone still has plenty of free space. The app reports a run that returned nothing as nothing.
