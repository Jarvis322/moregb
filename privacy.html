Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

More GB has no account, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect personal data, and the contents of your iPhone never leave it.

What the app reads

More GB reads two numbers that iOS publishes about the device volume: how much space is free, and how much the system reports it could release. It does this through Apple's own volumeAvailableCapacity APIs. It does not read, index, open or transmit your files, photos, messages or app data, and iOS would not permit it to.

These readings stay on the device. They are shown to you and then discarded.

What leaves your iPhone

One thing does, and only if you buy something.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details, card, or Apple Account.

To know whether a purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which validates the Apple receipt and stores a randomly generated, anonymous identifier for the installation together with the purchase and its status. That identifier is not linked to your name, email, phone number, contacts or advertising identifier, and we do not upload any of those. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking

None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no third-party trackers, it does not fingerprint the device, and it never asks for the App Tracking Transparency permission because it has nothing to track.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission, and only if you ask it to: notifications, when you turn on the monthly reminder in Settings. Leave that switch off and the app never prompts you for anything.

The reminder is scheduled on your device by iOS. It contains no personal data, is not sent from a server, and nothing about it leaves your iPhone.

Nothing else is ever requested: no photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing about you to access, correct or export. If you would like the anonymous purchase record associated with your installation deleted, email info@jarviswdesign.com and it will be removed; note that this cannot delete Apple's own record of your purchase, which is governed by Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the public history of this page's repository.
